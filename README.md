Mini Wonders Children's Physiotherapy Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
Mini Wonders Children's Physiotherapy is a specialist paediatric physiotherapy provider based in Chelmsford, Essex. The clinic supports babies, children and young people with neurological conditions, developmental challenges and rehabilitation needs through personalised physiotherapy programmes.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Mini Wonders as a specialist children's physiotherapy provider, strengthening brand credibility, improving service organisation and helping parents, caregivers and healthcare professionals find relevant treatment information.
2. Main Improvements
1. Corrected Branding and Contact Information
- Removed unrelated PaedsPhysio and Physio4You branding from the homepage.
- Replaced incorrect third-party contact details with Mini Wonders' verified telephone number and email address.
- Ensured contact and appointment pathways represent Mini Wonders rather than another organisation.
Problem addressed: The previous website displayed another company's branding, telephone number, email address and booking link, creating confusion and damaging trust.
2. Consistent Experience and Credibility Messaging
- Addressed the conflicting experience claims appearing across the homepage and About page.
- Removed inconsistent messaging that could create uncertainty about the team's experience.
- Improved the consistency of credibility-related content.
Problem addressed: Conflicting claims of over 30 years and over 10 years of experience could undermine parental confidence in the clinic.
3. Improved Homepage Structure and Service Organisation
- Corrected the homepage section sequence and missing section numbering.
- Reduced repeated value statements.
- Improved service descriptions to communicate specialist paediatric physiotherapy offerings more clearly.
- Structured information to help parents and healthcare professionals identify relevant services.
Problem addressed: Missing section numbering, repeated content and generic service descriptions made the previous homepage less organised and harder to navigate.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: Strengthened Mini Wonders' own brand identity by removing unrelated third-party branding.
- Homepage Layout: Improved section sequencing and content hierarchy to create a more consistent page structure.
- User Experience: Made contact information and specialist services easier to identify.
- Content Consistency: Addressed conflicting experience claims and repetitive messaging.
- Service Communication: Improved the presentation of specialist paediatric physiotherapy, intensive therapy and rehabilitation services.

Project Focus: Brand integrity, consistent credibility messaging and clearer specialist service presentation for parents, caregivers and healthcare professionals.
