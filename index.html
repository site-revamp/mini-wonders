What we offer

Specialist physiotherapy services

Our services bring together individualised therapy and specialist approaches to support children's movement and rehabilitation goals. The most suitable approach depends on each child's needs and assessment.

01 / SERVICE

Paediatric Physiotherapy

Individualised physiotherapy to support children's movement, physical development and functional goals.

02 / SERVICE

Intensive Therapy Programmes

Structured blocks of therapy designed around a child's assessment, goals and individual programme.

03 / SERVICE

Pre and Post SDR Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy support associated with preparation for and rehabilitation following Selective Dorsal Rhizotomy.

04 / SERVICE

Multidisciplinary Working

Working alongside other professionals where appropriate to support joined-up care and a child's wider needs.

05 / SERVICE

Community-Based Physiotherapy

Therapy options may include visits in home, school, gym or hospice settings, depending on individual arrangements.

06 / SERVICE

Specialist Therapy Approaches

Approaches and equipment may include DMI, TheraSuit, Spider Cage Therapy, gait training and electrical stimulation, when clinically appropriate.

Our approach
